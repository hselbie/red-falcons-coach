👶 7-Year-Old (U8) Soccer Coaching Philosophy

  • 1. Equal Playing Time for All: Every child who wants to play gets equal time. If a child wants a break, pause them without pressure.
  • 2. "Spread Out!" (Avoid the Beehive): 7-year-olds swarm the ball. Remind wingers to stay wide and defenders to protect the goal.
  • 3. Try Every Position: Rotate kids between Goalie, Defense, Midfield, and Striker over the season.
  • 4. Positive Reinforcement Only: Celebrate great passes, hustle, sportsmanship, and trying hard.
  • 5. Quick Whistle Rules:
    • No hands (except Goalie in the box).
    • Kick-ins or throw-ins depending on local league rules.
    • No slide tackles at this age.
    • Substitutions happen at half-time or on the fly when ball is out of bounds.
